What is tempbox.site?

tempbox.site provides you with a free, temporary, and anonymous email address. When you sign up for online services, forums, or download free files, websites often require an email address. Unfortunately, this usually leads to your real inbox being flooded with promotional spam, newsletters, and potential data breaches. By using our disposable inbox, you can receive verification codes and confirmation links without ever exposing your true identity.

Why use a temporary email address?

  • Stop Spam: Keep your personal and work email accounts completely clean and free of junk mail.
  • Protect Your Privacy: Prevent companies from tracking your online activities across multiple websites.
  • Test Safely: Developers and QA testers can generate multiple addresses instantly to test application workflows.
  • No Registration Required: Your temporary inbox is generated instantly in your browser the moment you visit our site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service totally free?

Yes! tempbox.site is 100% free to use. There are no hidden fees, premium tiers, or registration walls.

Can I send emails from this address?

No. To prevent abuse, spam, and malicious activity, our temporary email addresses are receive-only. You cannot reply to or compose outgoing messages.

How long do my emails stay in the inbox?

Emails and the temporary addresses themselves are ephemeral. Once you close your browser session or click "New address", the inbox and all associated messages are permanently deleted from the servers.
